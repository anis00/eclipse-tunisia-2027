**Eclipse in Tunisia 2027** is an independent information portal dedicated to the total solar eclipse of 2 August 2027 in Tunisia. It is intended for residents, visitors, amateur and professional astronomers, photographers, teachers, students and the media.

## Our commitments

- **Reliable astronomy**: all times and durations are computed by the site from NASA's Besselian elements, and the engine is validated against independent references. Methods, sources and known limitations are published on the [sources and methods](../sources/) page.
- **Safety first**: our observation advice follows the recommendations of NASA and of the American Astronomical Society.
- **Clear distinctions**: we separate computed astronomical data, climate statistics, practical travel information and recommendations. Information that may change is dated.
- **No invented content**: no fake reviews, no fake availability, no unverified travel times. The site does not recommend any commercial service.
- **Privacy by design**: no account, no tracking cookies; your position is used only in your browser. See the [privacy policy](../privacy/).

## Three languages

The site is published in English, French and Arabic, the languages most used by visitors and residents of Tunisia. All important pages, results and messages are translated.

## After the eclipse

The site is designed to evolve after 2 August 2027 into an archive of the event and an educational resource on solar eclipses and other astronomical events visible from Tunisia.
