## Purpose and liability

This site provides general information about the total solar eclipse of 2 August 2027. Astronomical predictions are computed with care and validated against reference sources, but they remain predictions with documented limits of accuracy (see [sources and methods](../sources/)). Practical travel information is given for guidance and must be checked with the relevant operators and authorities. Observing the Sun is dangerous without appropriate protection: follow the [safety guide](../guide/).
